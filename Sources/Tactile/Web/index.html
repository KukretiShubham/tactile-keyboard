Key Height

With Rest Fingers on, keys type when you lift your finger, so fingers resting on the keys don't type. Delete and the arrow keys still repeat when held.

Adapt to My Typing learns where you press each key and moves its touch area to match, on this iPad only. Match Mac Layout labels the keys for the keyboard layout your Mac uses, such as AZERTY.

Shortcuts

In landscape with the trackpad showing, shortcuts are keys on either side of the trackpad. Otherwise they sit along the top.

App Hotkeys

Beside the trackpad, hotkeys for the app you're using on your Mac (VS Code, Zed, Xcode, JetBrains, browsers) appear on their own. In a terminal, the Claude Code or Codex tab you picked last stays. Tapping a tab brings that app to the front on your Mac; Claude Code and Codex come back wherever you used them last.

Apps

Apps you switch off don't get a tab, even when they're in front on your Mac.

Trackpad

Pinch to Zoom zooms the app you're using, like ⌘+ and ⌘−. With Three-Finger Drag on, drag windows and text with three fingers; swiping between spaces then takes four.

If three-finger swipes show Undo or Redo on the iPad instead of switching Mac desktops, open the iPad's Settings → Multitasking & Gestures and turn off Productivity Gestures. Keep Three-Finger Drag off to switch Mac desktops with three fingers. This changes editing gestures across the iPad.
