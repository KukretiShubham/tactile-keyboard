Secure Your Connection

Tactile encrypts everything you type between this iPad and your Mac. To do that, this iPad needs to trust your Mac's certificate. You only do this once.

Skip this with Tailscale

Your Mac is on Tailscale. Install Tailscale on this iPad from the App Store and sign in with the same account. This page then opens Tactile by itself, with no certificate steps.

  1. 1

    Download the certificate

    Safari asks to download a configuration profile. Tap Allow.

    Download Certificate
  2. 2

    Install the profile

    Open Settings, tap Profile Downloaded near the top, then tap Install. Tap More Details first to compare its SHA-256 fingerprint with the one in Tactile’s menu on your Mac (hold Option while opening it):

    …
  3. 3

    Turn on full trust

    In Settings, go to General → About → Certificate Trust Settings and turn on Tactile CA.

  4. 4

    Open Tactile

    Waiting for this iPad to trust the certificate…

    Open Tactile

The certificate can only be used for local names and private network addresses, so it can't be used to intercept websites on the internet.
