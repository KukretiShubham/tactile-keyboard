# Code of conduct

Tactile is a small project made for people to enjoy. To keep it a place people want to come back to:

- **Be kind and patient.** People ask questions at every level of experience; answer the way you'd want to be answered.
- **Assume good intent,** and critique ideas and code, never people.
- **Stay on topic** in issues and pull requests, and respect people's time.
- **No harassment of any kind:** no insults, slurs, sexual content, threats, or personal attacks, and no publishing anyone's private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block people who don't follow this.

To report a problem privately, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, issue or pull request (the ··· menu), or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential.
